MEGALODON TOOTH STAND — print + assembly
=========================================
Two builds — pick the folder for your nozzle (same stand; only the joint fit and the engraved seal differ):
  nozzle_0.4/  seal CHISEL-CUT (45-deg V grooves, no bridged letter floors), Helvetica Neue Medium,
               1.2 mm strokes, 0.9 mm lands, bigger type, inch marks, smoothed tooth outline; 0.20 mm joint fit
  nozzle_0.2/  seal in Regular, finer 0.7 mm strokes; 0.15 mm joint fit
Parts in each (no supports on either):
  megalodon_stem_print_XX.stl  bell + rod   — stands on the bell mouth (engraved seal on the bed side)
  megalodon_halo_print_XX.stl  arc + clips  — upside-down on its flat top, pin pointing up

Gcode (PrusaSlicer, PLA, generic speeds — re-slice in your own profile for real times):
  0.4 nozzle / 0.2 layers:  stem ~5h25 78g  · halo ~53m 11g
  0.2 nozzle / 0.1 layers:  stem ~21h14 64g · halo ~3h19 9g
Rebuild: python megalodon_stand.py 0.4   (or 0.2) — needs megalodon_underside.py beside it.
Settings: 4 walls, gyroid, stem 12% / halo 30%, seam rear, elephant-foot comp ON (0.15 in the 0.4 profile).
IMPORTANT for the engraved bottom: bottom solid thickness >= 1.1 mm (the seal is 1.0 mm deep),
otherwise the letter walls sit in sparse infill. The included .ini profiles already do this.

Two-colour print on the P2S (added Oct 11): open megalodon_stem_2color_XX.3mf in Bambu Studio. It is a project
  file: P2S 0.4 nozzle, 0.20 mm Standard, 4 walls, 12% gyroid, prime tower on, and ONE object with two parts —
  "stem body" on filament 1 and "seal inlay" (rim rings, text, tooth drawing, weight) on filament 2, flush with the
  base. Map filament 1 to the base colour and 2 to the seal colour in the AMS panel, slice, print. Check the object
  list shows "seal inlay" with filament 2; if it ever shows 1, click the part and pick 2 (that is the only step).
  The colour change only happens in the first 1.0 mm (5 layers at 0.2) — about 10-15 g of purge and ~20 min, then
  single colour to the top. The halo is unchanged: print it from whichever spool you like.
  Fallback without the 3MF: import megalodon_stem_print_XX.stl AND megalodon_stem_inlay_print_XX.stl together,
  answer Yes to "load as a single object with multiple parts", set the inlay part to filament 2.
  Single-colour build: megalodon_stem_print_XX.stl still has the seal engraved 1.0 mm deep as before.

Magnet pockets (added Oct 11): two 5 mm x 2.8 mm deep pockets in the bed face, at +-37 mm either side of the
  tooth drawing on the 3/9 o'clock axis (in line with the rim dots). Bore 5.20 (0.4 nozzle) / 5.15 (0.2) with a
  0.3 mm lead-in; magnet sits flush with the base. Press in with a drop of CA. Roof over each pocket is 6 mm+ of
  bell, printed as a 5 mm bridge (no supports). Edit mag_* in megalodon_stand.py to move or resize them.

Assembly:
  1. Test-fit the halo's pin into the rod top (D-flat faces the tooth; the socket mouth is chamfered to guide it). Then glue it: thin CA
     on the pin AND the flat bearing face where the halo sits on the rod. Required — the pin
     carries the tooth's forward lean.
  2. Lower the tooth point-down until both edges seat in the clips.

Fit assumptions (edit megalodon_stand.py and re-run if needed):
  groove_span 100 mm  — tooth settles where it is 100 mm wide; measure where on your tooth that is.
  tip_z 45 mm         — tip height; ~31 mm clear over the bell on the assumed shape, ~12 mm worst case.
  clips take edge thickness up to ~10 mm.
Seal reads correctly when the stand is tipped over sideways.
